    Market microstructure: order-flow toxicity and trade-signing

    An empirical study of adverse selection in US equities.

    • Measured adverse selection on 10.7M US equity orders across 5 tickers and 29 days using post-trade markouts and block-bootstrapped confidence intervals.
    • Benchmarked Lee-Ready, tick, quote, and EMO rules against ground-truth labels; found errors concentrate in a narrow near-mid band where Lee-Ready's 98% accuracy hides a reversal of the market maker's “quote/no-quote” decision.
    • Showed the standard (1-2p) attenuation correction fails: non-uniform errors mean global shrinkage reprices unaffected states and understates true adverse selection exactly where market makers get picked off.
    • Python
    • Pandas
    • NumPy
